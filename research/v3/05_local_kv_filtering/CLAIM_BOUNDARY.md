# Claim boundary

Supported by reported evidence: three paired seed endpoints favor local K/V
at matched characters on this exact 47M recipe. Three reported checkpoint audits
reproduce dense validation. These records are transcriptions, not newly executed
or independently retrieved results. Checkpoint hashes identify external files.

Not established: superiority over competitors, universal scaling, statistical
certainty from three seeds, infinite memory, novel causal convolution, or equal
quality at equal wall-clock training budget. Seed1's large effect makes the mean
unrepresentative without individual results. The endpoint campaign was adaptive:
only seed3 was prospectively frozen after observing the first two endpoints.

The control already uses retention-scale512 and optimized execution. Do not
attribute all improvements over released v2 models to this filter. Runtime
ratios are median update measurements, not full-job speedups. Carry probes use
only65536 validation targets; they are not whole-split streaming BPC.

Checkpoint hashes are not durable hosting. Preserve all six files and original
notebook versions before claiming full artifact availability. No raw checkpoint
or dataset is included here. No release or default backend is promoted here.
