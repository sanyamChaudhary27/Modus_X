# Claim boundary

Supported: canonical per-byte archive decay was a measurable retention
bottleneck; changing only its timescale improved retained traces; archive state
causally reduced NLL on the measured natural consistent and conflicting repeat
protocol; no severe conflicting-band harm was observed.

Not supported: semantic fact revision, universal resistance to stale memory,
localization of individual facts inside the matrix, or a claim that 512 is the
globally optimal timescale.
